Privacy Policy for MarketAI

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Welcome to MarketAI ("we", "us", "our", or "the Application"), operated by Trusting Brains. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data and marketing assets you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of information when you use the MarketAI mobile application, web services, and associated backend APIs.

1. Information We Collect

To provide AI-assisted marketing, automated social scheduling, Meta campaign management, and competitor research, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Phone Number & Identity: Collected for secure One-Time Password (OTP) verification and authentication.
  • Business Profile Details: Your name, email address, business name, business address, industry type, services offered, and business logo.

B. Meta Platform Data (Facebook & Instagram)

When you connect your Facebook or Instagram Business accounts via official Meta OAuth Login, we request permissions to perform marketing actions on your behalf. We may receive:

  • Page & Account Information: Facebook Page IDs, Instagram Business Account IDs, Page names, access tokens, and profile avatars.
  • Publishing Permissions: Ability to schedule, upload images/videos, and publish content to your connected Facebook feeds and Instagram accounts (pages_manage_posts, instagram_content_publish).
  • Ads Management & Insights: Meta Ad Account IDs, campaign hierarchies (Campaigns, AdSets, Ads), targeting metadata, and real-time performance insights (spend, clicks, impressions, ROI metrics).
  • Lead Generation Data: Forms metadata and lead submissions (leads_retrieval) retrieved strictly to display within your secure MarketAI Leads Manager.

C. Artificial Intelligence Generation Inputs

  • Marketing Prompts: Business descriptions, product topics, selected tones of voice, and keywords provided to generate post captions, ad copy, and hashtags via the Google Gemini AI engine.

D. Payment & Recharge Information

  • Subscription Transactions: Plan type (e.g., Growth Pro, Semi-Annual, Annual Pass), duration days, purchase dates, and Razorpay transaction reference IDs.
  • Note: We do not collect or store credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs. All financial payments are securely processed by Razorpay Payment Solutions in compliance with PCI-DSS standards.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the following business purposes:

  • AI Creative Generation: Transmitting prompt criteria to Google Generative AI (Gemini) solely to generate high-performing marketing copy. Your prompts are never sold or used for model retraining by unauthorized third parties.
  • Publishing & Automated Scheduling: Executing background scheduling queues to publish your approved posts to connected Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts at the times you configure.
  • Meta Ads & Campaigns Deployment: Creating and updating advertising campaigns, setting budgets, targeting parameters, and pulling ROI performance reports.
  • Competitor Ad Spy & Analysis: Querying public advertising libraries and scraper tools (e.g., Apify / Meta Ad Library) to display public marketing trends and strategy breakdowns.
  • Subscription Management: Tracking active plan validity, expiration timelines, and quota enforcements.

3. How We Share & Disclose Information

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or Meta marketing credentials to advertisers or data brokers.

We only share data with essential third-party service providers required to deliver the application's core functionality:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: Via official Meta Graph APIs to execute posting, campaign management, and analytics queries.
  • Google Cloud / Gemini AI: To generate AI copy, headlines, and captions based on your input criteria.
  • Razorpay: To process secure subscription billing and renewals.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Database: Secure encrypted cloud servers hosting our Node.js and MySQL databases.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • Encryption & Security: All client-server communications are transmitted using industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption. Access tokens and authentication states are protected by JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • Data Retention: We retain your marketing data and account information as long as your account remains active. Scheduled posts that have been published or failed are archived in your account history until you delete them.
  • Meta Access Tokens: Stored securely in our encrypted database and can be revoked by you at any time.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion (Meta Platform Compliance)

You have full control over your personal data and connected social accounts:

  • Disconnecting Social Accounts: You can disconnect your Facebook or Instagram accounts directly from the MarketAI Social Connect screen or through your Facebook Account Settings under Settings & Privacy > Apps and Websites > MarketAI > Remove.
  • Requesting Full Account & Data Deletion: You have the right to request complete deletion of your account, posts, lead logs, and social tokens from our systems.

To request complete data deletion:

Please visit our dedicated Data Deletion Instructions Page or send an email to trustingbrains@gmail.com with your registered phone number. We will permanently delete all your account records, social tokens, and cached assets within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

MarketAI is intended for business marketing professionals and commercial users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.
