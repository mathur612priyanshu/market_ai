Meta Platform Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

According to Facebook Platform and Meta Developer Data Policies, MarketAI provides users with clear instructions on how to request the deletion of their personal data and revoke all Facebook/Instagram account permissions.

Method 1: Removing MarketAI from your Facebook Account

You can revoke MarketAI's access and remove all stored permissions directly from your Facebook profile by following these steps:

  1. Log into your Facebook Account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Permissions section and click on Apps and Websites.
  4. Find MarketAI in the list of connected apps.
  5. Click Remove next to MarketAI.
  6. Check the box to request Facebook to delete all historical permissions and posts created by the app, and click Remove to confirm.

Method 2: Request Complete Deletion from MarketAI Database

If you wish to permanently delete your MarketAI account, profile records, phone numbers, post history, leads, and API tokens from our database servers:

Send an email to: trustingbrains@gmail.com

Subject: Request for Data Deletion - MarketAI

Please include: Your registered mobile phone number and full name.

Our data protection team will verify your identity and permanently delete all your records, media, and access tokens within 48 hours, sending you a confirmation receipt.
